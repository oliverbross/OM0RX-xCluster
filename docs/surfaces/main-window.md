# Main window

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — A live DX operations board, not a generic dashboard. The spot stream is the first viewport and remains the dominant surface; configuration and explanation stay one action away.

**OWN-WORLD** — Near-black navy surfaces (`#07111f`, `#0b1728`, `#101f34`) with disciplined one-pixel blue-gray separators. Cyan frequency/callsign accents, emerald confirmation, and the established OM0RX status palette carry meaning. Native macOS controls are compact and squared rather than pill-heavy.

**STORY** — Connection and logbook truth are visible first. Filters reduce the stream without hiding their active state. A click explains a call through previous QSOs and the matrix; a double-click transfers it to RUMlogNG.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — A narrow connection rail, one horizontally scrollable row of color-coded multi-select filters, a full-width dense spot table, and a truthful selected-spot inspector anchored at the bottom. The callsign is the strongest datum, frequency second, status color third. No welcome panel or decorative empty cards displace the table.

**FORM** — Established OM0RX operator-console world adapted to native macOS. The supplied screenshots pin the palette, density, column order, and detail-sheet hierarchy, so no concept tournament is needed.

**MOTION** — Only operational state changes animate: new spots enter with a short highlight decay and connection indicators cross-fade. Reduced Motion removes both. Hover, focus, error, disconnected, empty, loading, and disabled states remain explicit.
